**[Naaleens](https://naaleens.com): a lens on every name.**
A professional health network in short vertical video, built at Deep Axiom.

Every post shows what its author is: patient, student, physician or institution. A physician's badge comes from their professional license, proven with the sealed certificate of Mexico's national registry, and unverified accounts show as unverified. When an account proves it practices, a clinical workspace opens inside the same app: visits, notes, prescriptions and scheduling, built on FHIR R4 and Mexico's NOM-004 and NOM-024.

- **Backend:** one Go module for the network and the clinical record, on PostgreSQL with row-level security.
- **Clients:** React 19 + TypeScript on the web and Flutter on mobile, both drawn from one declared contract (server-driven UI).
- **AI:** AURA, our own kernel written in Go. Nothing it proposes reaches a clinical record without a human approving it.
- **Languages:** Spanish, English and Brazilian Portuguese.

## What I work on

- **Computer vision:** detection models for medical imaging, histology and industrial inspection, deployed down to edge devices.
- **Systems:** concurrent, fault-tolerant backends in Go, Rust and Elixir.
